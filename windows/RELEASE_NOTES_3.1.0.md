WeatherFast 3.1.0 for Windows adds Places of the World: famous places around the world that you can browse, with each one's weather. It updates 3.0.2, and WeatherFast offers it to you the next time it starts.

## Added

- **Places of the World.** The Browse dialog has a new tab next to U.S. States and International, and the Cities menu has **Browse Places of the World...** to open straight on it. There are three lists to start:
  - **Famous Bridges:** 153 bridges, from the Golden Gate to the Millau Viaduct.
  - **Extreme Weather:** 150 places known for record heat, cold, wind, rain, snow and lightning, including mountain summits and Antarctic research stations.
  - **Beaches of the World:** 144 famous beaches on every continent.

  Choose a list and press **Load Places**. Each line gives the place's name and weather first, then where it is and a short note on why it's known, for example "Golden Gate Bridge - 55°F, Clear (High: 60°F, Low: 50°F). San Francisco, California, United States. Art Deco suspension bridge across the Golden Gate strait". US places also show their most serious weather alert, as US cities do.

  Sort a list by name, by direction or by temperature (the coldest summits are a sight). Check the places you want and choose **Add Selected Cities**: a place keeps its name in your list, for example "Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco". **Add List to Favorites** puts a list on the Favorites tab.

The same lists are in WeatherFast for iPhone and on the web at weatherfast.online.
