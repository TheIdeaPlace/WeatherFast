WeatherFast 3.1.0 for Windows adds Places of the World: lists of famous places around the world, each with its weather. It updates 3.0.2, and WeatherFast offers it to you the next time it starts.

## Added

- **Places of the World.** The Browse dialog has a new tab next to U.S. States and International. To open the dialog straight on it, choose **Browse Places of the World...** on the Cities menu. There are three lists to start:
  - **Famous Bridges:** 153 bridges, from the Golden Gate to the Millau Viaduct.
  - **Extreme Weather:** 150 places known for record heat, cold, wind, rain, snow and lightning, including mountain summits and Antarctic research stations.
  - **Beaches of the World:** 144 famous beaches on every continent.

  Choose a list and press **Load Places**. Each line gives the place's name and weather first, then where it is and a short note on why it's known. For example: "Golden Gate Bridge - 55°F, Clear (High: 60°F, Low: 50°F). San Francisco, California, United States. Art Deco suspension bridge across the Golden Gate strait". Places in the United States, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands also show their most serious weather alert, as US cities do.

  You can sort a list by name, by direction or by temperature; sorted cold to warm, Extreme Weather starts with the summits and the poles. Check the places you want and choose **Add Selected Cities**. A place keeps its name in your list, with its nearest town: for example, "Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco". **Add List to Favorites** keeps a list on the Favorites tab.

The same lists are in the WeatherFast for iPhone beta on TestFlight, and they're coming to the web version at weatherfast.online.
