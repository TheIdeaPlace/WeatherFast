New in this build: jump a year in Historical Weather

When you browse 30 consecutive days in Historical Weather, there are now Previous Year and Next Year buttons under the day arrows. They show the same 30 days in the year before or after, so you can compare a stretch of days across years without going back to the date picker.

What to test
- Open a city, open Historical Weather, load history, and choose Browse Days.
- Press Previous Year a few times, and check that the dates stay the same and only the year changes.
- Press Next Year, and check that it is dimmed when the next year's range would start today or later.
- Start a browse on February 29 of a leap year, then move a year. It should land on February 28.
- With VoiceOver, check that the buttons read "Previous year" and "Next year" and that their hints name the year they go to.
