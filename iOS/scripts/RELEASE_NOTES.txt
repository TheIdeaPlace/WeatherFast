New in this build

Places of the World: the Browse tab has a new entry next to United States and International. It opens curated lists of famous places around the world, and you can check the weather at any of them the same way you browse a state or a country.

Three lists to start:
- Famous Bridges: about 150 bridges, from the Golden Gate to the Millau Viaduct.
- Extreme Weather: about 150 places known for heat, cold, wind, rain, snow, lightning and more, including summits and Antarctic stations.
- Beaches of the World: about 145 famous beaches on every continent.

Each place shows its current weather, where it is, and a one-line note on why it's notable. Open one for the full forecast, or use the Add to My Cities action to save it. Saved places keep their name, for example "Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco". Lists can be sorted, searched by name or country, and added to Browse favorites with the star button.

What to test
- Browse, then Places of the World: open each list and check that weather loads.
- With VoiceOver, check that each place reads its name, weather, location and note, and that the Add to My Cities action works.
- Search a list by a country or state name, for example Japan or Hawaii.
- Open a few places and check the forecast looks right for where they are.
- Tell us about any place that seems to be in the wrong spot or has a wrong note.
