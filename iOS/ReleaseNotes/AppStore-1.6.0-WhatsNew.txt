***New***

Open at Launch: You can now choose a city for WeatherFast to open to instead of your city list. The app starts on that city's full weather, and Back takes you to your list. Choose Open at Launch from a city's Actions menu in Flat view, from the menu when you touch and hold a city in List view (also available as a VoiceOver action), or from the Actions menu on the city's own screen. My Location can be your choice too. Settings > Display Options > Open at Launch shows your current choice and lets you switch back to opening on the city list. The setting is kept separately on each device, so your iPhone and iPad can open to different cities, even with iCloud sync on.

Radar Preview (off by default): WeatherFast now includes an early, experimental animated radar for the United States and Canada. It is turned off while it's being refined. To try it, go to Settings, choose Developer Settings at the bottom, and turn on Radar Loop, then open any city's Actions menu and choose Radar. You can choose National Weather Service, U.S. composite or Environment Canada radar, and browse radar stations from the Browse tab. Each frame is a real image, so VoiceOver's Intelligent Image Description in iOS 27 can describe it.
